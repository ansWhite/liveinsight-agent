# Demo Phone Pro

Demo Phone Pro is positioned as a value-focused phone under 3000 RMB.

## Selling Points

- 50MP main camera suitable for daily photos and night scenes.
- 5000mAh battery for long daily use.
- 6.7 inch OLED screen with smooth visual performance.

## Suitable Users

- Users who care about camera quality and battery life.
- Users with a budget around 2500 to 3000 RMB.

## Limitations

- Not designed for professional mobile gaming.
- Telephoto performance is limited compared with flagship models.
